# Brand Identity

## Public Line

A home for Originals.

## Internal Line

The Firm.

## Brand Behaviors

- selective
- controlled
- materially aware
- socially magnetic
- not mass-market
- not noisy

## Visual System

Core palette:

- Void Black: `#0B0B0C`
- Stark White: `#F5F3EE`
- Walnut: `#6B4C35`
- Deep Walnut: `#4B3323`
- Signature Orange: `#F56A1C`

Usage:

- one primary mark per surface
- orange as signal, not flood
- minimal product branding
- high material contrast
- strong invitation and packaging discipline

## Packaging Rule

Expensive theater belongs to the seeded circle, not every order.

Seeded premium packaging should stay limited to 15-20 people max for Drop 001.
