# Barrel Jeans - Zara Direction And Demand Scan

Checked: 2026-06-25

## Decision Supported

Choose the silhouette direction for `VE-BJ-001` before updating the supplier PDF and image/sketch pack.

## Conclusion

Use a Zara-inspired relaxed barrel fit as the new reference direction:

- unisex/menswear-readable block
- loose through hip and thigh
- bowed outer leg volume
- knee pleat/dart shaping
- full-length or slight stack length
- relaxed hem opening, not ankle-tight
- rigid or low-stretch cotton denim

Do not use the earlier Uniqlo direction as the primary silhouette. The Uniqlo pair is useful for public measurement structure, but it reads too tapered at the bottom for the current design intent.

## Product Direction To Use Next

Use this as the new pack direction:

- `VE-BJ-001`: unisex relaxed barrel jean.
- Rise: mid-to-high, depending on fit model testing.
- Fit: loose hip and thigh; curved barrel leg; generous knee volume; mild narrowing only below calf; relaxed hem opening.
- Hem rule: no tight ankle, no jogger effect, no skinny taper.
- Shape tools: knee pleats/darts allowed; hem dart allowed only if it does not create a tight cuff.
- Color priority: washed black / void black first; worn light blue or indigo second if MOQ allows.
- Construction identity: five-pocket jean, front button or zip/button, back yoke, back patch pocket, optional VORG-EAVY back patch/tag.
