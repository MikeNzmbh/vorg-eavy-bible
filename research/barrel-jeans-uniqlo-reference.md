## Known From the Public Product Page

Reference product: Uniqlo Barrel Jeans, product family E479000; the page displayed product ID 487973 on the date checked.

- Marketed as unisex.
- Loose fit, tapered silhouette, high rise, and pockets.
- Described as softly curved with volume at the sides.
- Regular inseam shown as 31 inches for sizes 22-34.
- Published compositions vary by product/color. The page lists 79% cotton / 21% lyocell for several colors, including 04 Gray and 68 Blue under product ID 487973.
- Published care: machine wash cold on gentle cycle; do not dry clean; do not tumble dry.
- Public garment chart fields: waist (relaxed), hip width, thigh, rise length, bottom width, and inseam.

Source: https://www.uniqlo.com/ca/en/products/E479000-000/00
