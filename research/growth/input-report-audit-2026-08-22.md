## What the reports did well

1. They separated creative taste from content volume. The useful system is recognizable world-building, recurring formats, founder proof, UGC and product evidence—not indiscriminate posting.
2. They treated Instagram as more than Reels: carousels, Stories, DMs, saves and the visual grid each have different jobs.
3. They gave TikTok a native hook/body/close grammar and treated search, creators and LIVE/shop behavior as commerce surfaces.
4. They made YouTube the long-form proof layer, with short clips cut from founder-led product truth.
5. They turned one shoot into a content inventory and used VHS texture as an accent rather than a substitute for clean garment evidence.
6. They connected creator seeding to contracts, rights, disclosure, codes, paid amplification and customer quality.
7. They moved the ecommerce discussion past ROAS into PDP continuity, contribution margin, returns, fit, shipping, checkout and inventory-aware marketing.
8. They added the major quiet channels: Google free listings, Pinterest, Shopify/Shop, email, SMS, referrals, post-purchase, reviews, loyalty, AI/product search and VORGEAVY as an owned discovery surface.
9. They correctly warned against adding each platform's claimed conversions together.
10. They proposed a measurement hierarchy and a launch story arc instead of one launch-day spike.
