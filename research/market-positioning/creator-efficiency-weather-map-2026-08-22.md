## Efficient creator tier for VORG (C$5–6k production)

Industry priors consistently say **nano (≈1k–10k) and micro (≈10k–100k)** beat mega on engagement cost for DTC fashion. Mega is awareness theatre VORG cannot afford and cannot measure honestly yet.
