## Ingestion Contract

```text
register source + rights basis
-> distill atomic claim
-> fingerprint and deduplicate mechanism
-> map to conditions, metric, threshold, kill rule, risk, and tactic
-> admit as hypothesis
-> run prerequisite-cleared VORG experiment
-> store evidence and adopt / adapt / reject / retest decision
```

The ingestion routes are deliberately different:

| Rights route | Local treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Public domain | Full text may be downloaded, indexed, quoted within editorial reason, and distilled with edition-level citation |
| Explicit open licence | AI-assisted full text may be processed only when the named edition's licence and current publisher terms permit the intended commercial and AI use; otherwise use manual reading and attributed citation |
| Open research | Process under the paper's stated license; retain citation and avoid representing a preprint as settled causal proof |
| Creator-provided free / free opt-in | Link and distill team-accessed material; do not republish the resource, subscriber archive, or creator's protected expression |
| Public-permitted page, tool, or ad library | Store URL, date, metadata, observations, and short factual notes; do not mirror protected pages or bypass access controls |

No pirated books, course rips, paywall bypasses, login-gated scraping, rate-limit evasion, private-community copying, address harvesting, or deleted/protected-post recovery.

## AI Rule

AI is useful for lawful transcription, classification, semantic clustering, contradiction checks, and first-draft test cards. It is not an evidence source by itself.

Ten creators repeating a tactic may still represent one underlying claim. The registry fingerprints the mechanism and collapses repetition into a single atomic claim. Repetition can raise research priority; it cannot raise Drop readiness, campaign proof, or the claim's evidence tier.

Only a completed VORG experiment with a valid receipt can move the Edge component of the algorithm. A planned claim, source, tactic, or AI-generated variant contributes zero.

## Known

- The registry currently validates 83 sources and 34 atomic claims against the 30-tactic Edge catalog.
- `full-text-allowed` is restricted to public-domain records; `full-text-license` is restricted to an explicit open licence whose current terms permit the intended AI-assisted commercial use.
- OpenStax Principles of Marketing is manual-read/cite because its current notice prohibits LLM or generative-AI ingestion without permission; the Red & Yellow text is manual-read/cite because its CC BY-NC-SA licence is not a blanket commercial reuse grant.
- Source IDs, claim IDs, URLs, rights routes, evidence tiers, tactic mappings, and semantic fingerprints have automated validation.
- The Drop OS Free library can browse and filter both source and claim records.

## Working Assumptions

- The evidence tiers and confidence scores are internal research triage, not external benchmarks.
- Public availability today does not guarantee permanent availability or unrestricted copying.
- Platform and vendor material is useful for mechanisms but can reflect commercial incentives.

## Unresolved

- The 83-source registry is a maintained seed portfolio, not “all free resources.” Coverage should expand when a source adds a genuinely new mechanism, stronger evidence, or a missing market angle.
- Protected works have not been ingested in full. They require founder-supplied, purchased, licensed, or library access.
- DTC Midas's complete X history remains unavailable without a lawful full-archive route or creator export; public fragments must not be described as all tweets.
- No source or claim has proven transfer to VORG-EAVY. Drop 001 outcome data is still required.
