# Commerce Library And Algorithm Audit

Checked: 2026-07-22

## Decision Supported

Decide whether the free commerce library and Drop OS scoring engine are strong enough to generate aggressive launch hypotheses without letting guru repetition, AI output, imported records, or weak receipts unlock Drop 001 production spend.

## Verdict

Conditionally approved for research triage and controlled proof tests. Not approved as a sales predictor, autonomous tactic executor, proof that any guru tactic transfers to VORG-EAVY, or a substitute for vendor quotes, approved samples, unit economics, Quebec launch compliance, and observed Drop 001 outcomes.

The material algorithm gaps found in this audit were fixed in `VORG Drop OS score v1.2` and covered by regression tests. The remaining limits are mostly evidence-collection and calibration limits, not reasons to weaken the current gates.

## What Was Evaluated

- Canonical free-source registry, rights routes, atomic claims, semantic fingerprints, generated browser bundle, and Free library UI.
- Edge tactic catalog, experiment state model, approval/prerequisite controls, evidence-tier weighting, spend limits, and decision memory.
- TypeScript scoring source, compiled browser bundle, unit tests, and real-browser operating flow.
- Open-library routes for public-domain and open-licensed books.
- Current Canadian and Quebec primary sources relevant to an Ottawa/Gatineau DTC launch.

## Known

### Library

- The registry contains 83 checked free-access routes, 34 deduplicated atomic claims, 15 commerce lenses, 7 public-domain books, and 3 open-licensed textbooks.
- Source volume contributes zero readiness. A source or claim becomes positive campaign proof only after a qualifying VORG experiment produces a linked result.
- AI-assisted full-text ingestion is allowed only for the identified public-domain edition or an explicit item-level licence whose current terms permit the intended AI and commercial use. A free preview, borrow button, search result, library listing, or generic Creative Commons label is not enough by itself.
- OpenStax Principles of Marketing is retained for manual reading and citation, but its current notice prohibits LLM/generative-AI ingestion without permission. The CC BY-NC-SA Red & Yellow text is also manual-read/cite for VORG unless the planned reuse is confirmed noncommercial or separately authorized.
- The generated browser bundle is tested for exact deep equality with the canonical registry, so stale or hand-edited generated data fails validation.

### Algorithm fixes completed

| Finding | Prior failure mode | v1.2 control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premature imported decision | `planned + adopt` could be interpreted as completed | Only explicit `status: completed` counts; an earlier decision is an evidence violation |
| Self-declared evidence tier | Unknown imported tactic could claim A/B/C | Only a catalog match verifies provenance; otherwise effective tier is F |
| Same-save approval | Operator could add approval while switching directly to running/completed | Yellow/Orange approval must already be named, timestamped, and saved before the run transition |
| Vacuous prerequisites | Empty checklist passed JavaScript `every()` | Positive proof requires a populated and fully cleared prerequisite checklist |
| Duplicate cross-surface receipt | One receipt could count once in legacy tactics and again in Edge | Unique normalized evidence references determine combined campaign-proof depth |
| Unknown risk | Missing/invalid imported risk could default Green | Unknown risk now fails closed to Orange |

## Working Assumptions

- Evidence tiers, scoring weights, blend ratios, and thresholds are internal operating policy, not universal ecommerce benchmarks.
- A syntactically valid proof link confirms that a reference was supplied; it does not inspect the artifact's truth or quality.
- The open-source portfolio is broad enough to create varied hypotheses, but only actual VORG observations can calibrate transferability.
- October 2026 remains a conditional acceleration case only; the working launch window is November 5-12, 2026 and still depends on production, inbound, QC, finance, campaign-proof, and operations gates.

## Unresolved

1. DTC Midas's complete X history is not lawfully available through the current public routes. Do not describe fragments, search results, or third-party thread indexes as all tweets.
2. Protected books and paid courses remain citation-only until the founder supplies a purchased, licensed, borrowed, or creator-authorized copy.
3. Proof links are not yet content-hashed, inspected, timestamped at capture, or expired when stale.
4. No real Drop 001 result set exists for backtesting v1.2 thresholds or evidence-tier multipliers.
5. The public `site/index.html` lookbook still contains the older three-object, September 2026 concept and is explicitly not launch-current.
6. Open-library URLs and licence terms can change; item-level rights need rechecking before every new full-text ingestion.
