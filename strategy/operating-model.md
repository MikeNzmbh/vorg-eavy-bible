# Operating Model

## Business Model

VORG-EAVY should launch as a direct-to-consumer fashion micro-label.

Do not start as a broad marketplace.

## First Cycle

- Channel: DTC only.
- Geography: Ottawa/Gatineau first.
- Catalog: 3 SKUs only.
- Launch format: password-gated store plus one physical event.
- Cash rule: reinvest first, withdraw later.
- Salary rule: load founder salary into economics, but preserve cash until the next PO is funded.

## Proof Loop

```text
Media -> Access pressure -> Sales -> Cash + Data + UGC -> Better product -> Stronger launch
```

## Why Narrow

Narrowing keeps:

- seeding dense
- paid media local
- FOMO visible
- operations learnable
- inventory risk capped
- founder attention focused

## January Gate

January 2027 is the decision gate. It is not a dramatic quit-or-fail moment.

If the gates are not met, keep the job, keep contributing monthly capital, and continue compounding.
