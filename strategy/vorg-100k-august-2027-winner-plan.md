# VORG Winner Plan — The 40-Day Compounding House

Checked: 2026-08-22
Decision: the integrated route to a C$100,000 August 2027 net-sales month.
Authority: goal-seeking plan; every scale step remains receipt-gated.
Market: U.S. primary; Brooklyn operating lead hypothesis; Chicago, Los Angeles and Atlanta co-finalists; Canada expands Ontario → Quebec → Vancouver → Halifax.

## The answer

VORG should run an **eight-release, 40-day compounding house** built around one coherent world, one hero/core replenishment spine, seasonal newness, disciplined filler and a diversified demand portfolio.

The business plans operational capacity to **50% growth per release**, but purchase-order authority uses a **45% floor** and only unlocks after the preceding release reaches **85% settled sell-through inside 30 days**, retains positive contribution, and clears cash, size, supplier and channel gates.

The 45% floor path produces approximately **C$112,387 August net sales**. The 50% target path produces approximately **C$142,331**, creating protection against a slower launch curve. The final modeled release lands 15 August 2027.

If the founder needs the full C$3,000 monthly company cash envelope from launch, ring-fence approximately **C$11,465** for the 45% path or **C$12,258** for the 50% path. Otherwise use a reinvestment-first cash gate and record unpaid months as deferred founder compensation. Dividends stay locked through month 12 and still require accountant/solvency clearance.

## Why this route wins

1. It begins with the exact August outcome and solves backward for dates, orders and capacity.
2. It gives Instagram an independent job beside TikTok, not a recycled-content afterthought.
3. It adds the quiet compounding channels—Google, Pinterest, Shop, email/SMS, search, referral and post-purchase.
4. It separates brand attention from transaction truth and transaction truth from retained contribution.
5. It imports useful systems from hedge funds, operations research and causal marketing without pretending VORG has enterprise data.
6. It protects founder pay and dividends from consuming inventory cash.
7. It treats comparable-company growth as a mechanism library, never a VORG receipt.
8. It can learn: frozen forecasts are compared with outcomes; negative evidence can shrink or reverse a bet.

## The eight-release arc

### Release 1 — Proof of product and tracking · 8 November 2026

Purpose: prove that the five-piece house reads as one world and that real customers complete the measured journey.

- 126-unit working architecture; production ceiling remains C$5,000-C$6,000.
- Hero: Firm Jacket. Core: women's and men's denim. Entry: scarf. Seasonal/feminine tension: top/bodysuit.
- Build one clean Shopify truth layer, U.S. USD path, product feed, consented CRM and source ledger.
- Founder content explains product decisions, not founder ambition in the abstract.
- The pop-up remains permission/market-proof gated.
- Day-30 scale gate: 85% settled sell-through, positive contribution, size/return read and next-PO cash.

### Release 2 — Repeat signal · 18 December 2026

Purpose: demonstrate that the first result can repeat without copying the first campaign.

- Replenish proven hero/core sizes; do not expand every weak cell.
- Use gift/holiday intent carefully without broad discount dependence.
- Activate Google free listings and evergreen Pinterest/catalog content.
- Begin first-order cohort and post-purchase fit/UGC system.
- Keep filler within 10% of the buy.

### Release 3 — Cohort and fit · 27 January 2027

Purpose: prove repeat behavior, improve size curves and establish a predictable creative rhythm.

- First returning-customer target becomes visible.
- Pool related SKU/size evidence cautiously; do not hide article differences.
- Creator renewal uses customer quality and delivery reliability.
- Cash-gated salary or protected bridge remains explicit.

### Release 4 — Spring world · 8 March 2027

Purpose: add seasonal newness without breaking the house.

- New piece must share image, styling, fit and margin grammar.
- YouTube becomes a durable discovery/proof library.
- Begin controlled creative/creator exploration only if attribution observations are sufficient.
- Confirm supplier reorder and QC can survive the next scale.

### Release 5 — Direct demand · 17 April 2027

Purpose: increase the share of people who seek VORG by name or return through owned channels.

- Brand/direct/search and returning-customer share should rise.
- Paid spend cannot be the only source of the growth step.
- Introduce referral/cultural participation with transparent benefits.
- Test a controlled physical proof moment only if local receipts/permissions clear.

### Release 6 — Operational scale · 27 May 2027

Purpose: prove service, fulfilment and creator/content capacity at several times launch volume.

- Formal first-hire/contractor trigger for operations or content execution.
- Daily stock-by-size, return and customer-service control.
- Split-wave PO or reserved material capacity becomes preferred.
- Incrementality testing may begin only with a credible control.

### Release 7 — July runway · 6 July 2027

Purpose: create the qualified audience, inventory and creative evidence that make August eligible.

- July is the dress rehearsal, not a disposable pre-launch.
- Build August waitlist by product, size, market and source.
- Freeze August demand range, channel-order ledger, content matrix and cash waterfall.
- No August buy if July fails the 85%/contribution/supplier gates.

### Release 8 — The August month · 15 August 2027

Purpose: recognize at least C$100,000 reconciled August net sales while preserving the next cycle.

- 45% floor path: about 1,698 inventory units / 1,155 30-day orders / C$131,237 30-day net sales; modeled August month about C$112,387.
- 50% target path: about 2,153 inventory units / 1,465 30-day orders / C$166,404 30-day net sales; modeled August month about C$142,331.
- The August dashboard reports net sales, not tax or platform-attributed overlap.
- Customer service, returns and September cash matter as much as launch-day screenshots.

## Product machine

### Assortment roles

- **Hero:** makes the brand recognizable and creates founder/product story depth.
- **Core:** earns replenishment from repeated fit/size demand.
- **Entry/belonging:** lowers first-purchase friction without cheapening the house.
- **Seasonal:** creates reason to return and cultural timing.
- **Filler:** plugs a genuine outfit/price/use gap; never hides weak hero demand.

### Buy architecture after proof

- 65–75% proven hero/core replenishment.
- 15–25% seasonal newness.
- 0–10% filler/entry experimentation.
- Risk-adjusted quantity is chosen by the demand range, margin, salvage, stockout/lost-customer cost and working-capital ceiling.
- Size decisions use settled units, return reasons, stockout views and waitlists by size.

## Prediction engine

### What runs now

- Source-quality/recency-weighted cross-signal nowcast.
- Google Trends saved query/geo/time exports as relative search direction only.
- Comparable-brand mechanism cards.
- Supplier, macro, tariff and market-entry state.
- Bottom-up order/capacity math.
- Base/up/down cash and demand scenarios.
- Fractional evidence-sized bets and pre-committed kill rules.

### What activates after receipts

| Trigger | Engine added | Decision improved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30+ settled orders + one full curve | Bayesian update + product-age sell-through hazard | Conversion/return range and timing |
| 2 drops + 100+ orders | Hierarchical pooling + newsvendor + cohort analysis | SKU/size quantities and repeat quality |
| 300+ attributable orders | Constrained Thompson sampling + calibration work | Creative/creator allocation with 10–20% exploration |
| Credible holdout/control | CausalImpact / geo or audience lift | Incremental orders and CAC |
| 52+ weekly observations with spend variation | Bayesian MMM + reconciled ensemble | Cross-channel budget portfolio |

No model is activated because it sounds intelligent. It activates when the data contract can support it.

## Finance machine

### Four ledgers

1. **Demand:** daily units/net sales by SKU-size and product age.
2. **Contribution:** net sales minus landed cost, shipping, fulfilment, payment, creator commissions and variable acquisition.
3. **Cash:** bank, protected tax/refund cash, PO commitments, salary, debt/shareholder funding and floor.
4. **Cohort:** acquisition source, first/returning, AOV, repeat time, returns and cumulative contribution.

### Salary and dividend policy

- C$3,000/month is the working total cash envelope, not confirmed net/gross salary.
- Cash-gated route protects the next PO first.
- Bridge-funded route protects monthly pay with C$11.5k–C$12.3k additional documented working capital.
- No salary, shareholder loan or owner contribution is revenue.
- No dividend before month 12; no automatic dividend at month 12.

## Tax/legal machine

- Canada export zero-rating is evidence-based, not assumed.
- Brooklyn pop-up triggers a specific New York sales-tax/permit review distinct from remote ecommerce thresholds.
- U.S. state nexus tracker runs monthly by sales, transactions, inventory, people/creator/agent and event activity.
- DDP vs U.S. 3PL, importer of record, HTS/origin/duty and returns are P0 contribution gates.
- Founder salary gets payroll/T4 review; dividend gets T5/classification/solvency review.
- FTC creator disclosure, commercial music, model/content rights, privacy, CASL and U.S. messaging consent are preflight gates.

## Brand mechanism library

- **Scuffers:** rapid product/store response, daily data rhythm, direct traffic and online proof before retail. Do not copy its 225% result.
- **Chubbies:** coherent lifestyle world, customer content and owned audience. Do not import its 50% growth rate.
- **Represent / founder brands:** founder media and product ladder. Do not assume founder visibility equals conversion.
- **Corteiz:** participatory city energy and real earned proof. Do not copy uncontrolled surprise crowds or scarcity theatre.
- **Telfar:** time-boxed preorder as demand/cash-information mechanism. Do not accept cash without explicit delivery/returns obligations.
- **Hermès/Jacquemus/Nude Project/Jaded/EME:** craft/price discipline, coherent world, community ritual, hero silhouette and drop-as-event. Study the mechanism, not peer-scale revenue.

## Failure modes and designed fixes

| Failure | Early signal | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traffic without orders | Reach rises, qualified PDP/checkout flat | Tighten buyer/problem/creative/PDP continuity; do not buy more reach |
| Orders without contribution | ROAS looks good, returns/fees erase margin | Kill scale; fix fit, price, shipping, channel fee or creator commission |
| Aggregate sell-through hides dead sizes | Hero sells, selected sizes age | SKU-size gate, fit correction, smaller tails, lost-demand capture |
| Founder salary starves PO | Bank falls below next commitment | Cash gate or protected bridge; no sales-tax cash |
| 45% inventory without 45% demand | More stock, same qualified audience | Hold scale and build channel-order coverage first |
| Creative fatigue | CPM/CTR/hold rate deteriorate; repeats rise | New franchise cells, creator rotation, founder/product proof |
| Platform shock | One channel >25% of acquisition | Shift into owned CRM, direct/search, Pinterest/Shop and creators |
| Supplier misses 40-day cadence | Sample/QC/reorder dates slip | Split waves, material reservation, alternate capacity, delay rather than fake stock |
| Filler dilutes brand | Entry items sell but hero/direct demand weakens | Filler ≤10%; brand/fit/margin gate; kill noisy pieces |
| Forecast becomes vanity | Inputs changed after outcome | Freeze call, version updates, publish error and negative receipts |
