The first money decision

Choose one shipping door.

CA→US

Ship from Canada with duties handled before delivery. Get the carrier quote, brokerage rules, return path, and tested customer promise.

US▣

Door B: U.S. 3PL

Move stock into a U.S. warehouse. Price inbound freight, storage, pick/pack, returns, inventory risk, and importer responsibility.

No door choice = no trustworthy U.S.-dollar margin.
