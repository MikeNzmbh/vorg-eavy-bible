## Phases

### Phase 0 — Proof buy (now → first sell-through)

**Borrow:** Hermès discipline (no fake luxury claims) + Jacquemus world coherence (tight image code) + Scuffers/Jaded organic-first content.

**Must clear:** DDP vs 3PL + USD sheet; GST/HST/CASL for CA; fit samples; claim ladder; nano/founder content with receipts.

**Budget:** Production ≤ C$6,000. Test cash separate, stage-gated. Mega creators blocked.

**Kill:** No bulk without PP sample. No paid scale without USD economics. No peer-name marketing.

### Phase 1 — Repeatable drop (Drop 002–003)

**Borrow:** Nude Project community ritual (owned media, not only ads) + Jaded hero-silhouette compounding + EME slower monthly cadence once quality holds.

**Must clear:** ≥1 drop with measured sell-through + contribution; return-reason loop; Drop 002 assortment grammar; trademark clearance packet.

**Budget:** Reinvest contribution first; raise production only with founder decision + quotes.

**Kill:** If Drop 001 contribution negative after returns, shrink SKU count before “brand house” spend.

### Phase 2 — Brand house (multi-market, selective retail)

**Borrow:** Nude/EME/Jaded pop-up→permanent only after queues/data; Jacquemus selective retail; Hermès full-price + craft control.

**Must clear:** Two proof drops; owned analytics; IP filings sequence; CS/3PL maturity; team hire #1 defined.

**Budget:** Separate opex from inventory; no wholesale until DTC story is recognizable.

**Kill:** Permanent lease or wholesale before contribution + brand recognition proxies.
