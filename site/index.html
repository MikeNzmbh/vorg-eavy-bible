Artifact Lifecycle

About

The Firm

The Idea

VORG-EAVY is a direct-to-consumer fashion micro-label. A home for Originals. Built selective, controlled, and culturally charged — where luxury restraint meets youth access pressure.

The Model

One city first. One drop architecture. One pop-up. One reinvestment loop until demand is proven. Ottawa and Gatineau are the first wedge.

Drop 001

Three objects. 150 units. C$295 / C$95 / C$65. Open online drop. One day-to-night pop-up. September 2026.

This is a proof machine, not a mature fashion label. Proof before scale.

The Release

The first drop opens online at release time with controlled units. Waitlist members receive priority updates and pop-up invitation signals.
