# On-set compositing capture checklist

## Before talent arrives

- Confirm usage rights, model release, location permission, and product approval.
- Lock delivery aspect ratios and minimum framing margin.
- Photograph gray card/color reference under the working light.
- Record lens, focal length, camera height, distance, aperture, shutter, ISO, and white balance.

## For each setup

- RAW hero frames.
- Clean plate without talent or product.
- Shadow/reference frame.
- Light-placement reference photo.
- Neutral/chrome reference when available.
- Focus plates and background plate.
- Extra framing for crops and motion.
- Garment construction, wrinkle, hardware, and label details.
- Product close-ups and alternate rotations.

## Before wrap

- Verify focus at 100 percent.
- Verify clean plates match the locked camera.
- Check highlight clipping on skin, white products, hardware, and glossy surfaces.
- Confirm no required garment detail or label was missed.
- Back up to two locations and preserve original RAW files.
