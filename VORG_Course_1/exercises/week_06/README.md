# Week 6 - Color and campaign consistency

Decision: make multiple images share one controlled visual world.

## Work

1. Neutral-balance three frames before building a look.
2. Protect skin, white products, black denim, and burgundy.
3. Build one restrained look with labeled layers or Resolve nodes.
4. Use waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope, and visual comparison together.
5. Save matched gallery stills and record exceptions.

## Deliverable

VORG look-development sheet, three matched images, and the completed Resolve node-tree or Photoshop grading template.

Pass: consistent contrast and color relationships without identical exposure or destructive clipping.
