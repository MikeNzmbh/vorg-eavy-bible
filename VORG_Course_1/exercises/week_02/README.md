# Week 2 - Photography and RAW source control

Decision: capture a plate that can survive compositing.

## Work

1. Reconstruct the selected Jacquemus composition with consenting talent and your own wardrobe or garment.
2. Record focal length, camera height, subject distance, aperture, shutter, ISO, and light placement.
3. Capture RAW hero frames, clean plate, gray card, shadow reference, garment details, and extra framing.
4. Develop nondestructively in Camera Raw. Do not retouch yet.
5. Compare crop, perspective, hierarchy, and shadow direction to the chosen source.

## Deliverable

`Week_02_Photography_Test.pdf`: contact sheet, metadata, lighting diagram, selected RAW development, and gap analysis.

Pass: matched composition and camera logic with no claim of exact original production settings.
