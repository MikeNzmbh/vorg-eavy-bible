# Week 3 - Premium shine and reflection design

Decision: make highlights describe material and form instead of adding generic gloss.

## Work

1. Light one glossy object using a large white card, narrow white strip, and black card.
2. Produce hard-sun, broad-soft, and narrow-strip versions.
3. Test black denim texture and a white product edge against controlled backgrounds.
4. Label highlight shape, reflected source, shadow edge, and material cue.
5. Diagnose shiny, plastic, wet, metallic, and premium-glossy differences.

## Deliverable

`Week_03_Shine_Lab.pdf`: nine-frame contact sheet, diagrams, and a one-page material-read critique.

Pass: at least three visibly different controlled highlight shapes with preserved surface texture.
