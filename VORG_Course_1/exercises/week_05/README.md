# Week 5 - Photoshop integration and retouching

Decision: make separate elements feel photographed together while protecting the real garment and person.

## Work

1. Use the supplied layer-stack template.
2. Separate contact, cast, and ambient shadows.
3. Match perspective, values, local color, depth, edge contamination, and grain.
4. Preserve skin, seam, weave, hardware, and wrinkle truth.
5. Check the composite in color, grayscale, at 100 percent, and at final output size.

## Deliverable

`Week_05_Compositing_Breakdown.psd`, flattened master, before/after, and labeled breakdown.

Pass: no obvious cutout edge, floating object, conflicting highlight, texture loss, or product-truth change.
