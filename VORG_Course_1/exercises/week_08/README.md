# Week 8 - Original VORG capstone campaign

Decision: prove the learned visual principles can create original VORG work.

## Work

Produce six related images from VORG-owned or licensed sources:

1. Model hero with one controlled CGI idea.
2. Product-only still life.
3. Macro texture or construction image.
4. Canadian environmental image.
5. Clean commercial image.
6. Controlled experimental image.

Adapt approved masters to portrait, square, desktop hero, mobile hero, story, poster, and email banner. Include before/after and labeled breakdowns.

## Deliverable

Six masters, required adaptations, source/rights log, contact sheet, breakdowns, and final 100-point rubric.

Pass: 85 or higher, no major realism or product-truth failure, and no close brand replica presented as original work.
