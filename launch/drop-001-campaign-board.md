## Campaign Position

Drop 001 should feel like:

```text
The Firm arrives in Ottawa/Gatineau first.
The product is controlled, but the online launch is open.
The proof is in the objects, the city, and the people wearing them.
```

## Non-Negotiables

- No fake proof.
- No fake consumption.
- No fake crowds.
- No password gate at online launch.
- No unsafe surprise rush mechanics.
- No public-space tactics without permission.
- No creator or customer content without consent/disclosure where needed.

## Primary Campaign Bet

The winning campaign angle is not just "look at the clothes."

The winning angle is:

```text
VORG-EAVY treats the first drop like a serious object system:
fit explained, fabric shown, city activated, proof captured.
```

## Sample Proof Gate

Do not move to full campaign build unless:

- founder can explain each item in under 60 seconds
- at least 3 sample posts produce qualified product questions
- fit objections are known and answerable
- visuals make the product look worth its price
- waitlist/SMS or DM intent rises after product education

## The Exchange v0

Working concept:

```text
Bring a clean black jacket, structured top, cap, or useful garment.
Receive a VORG-EAVY fitting priority card, alteration/repair credit, or controlled purchase priority.
Collected items are donated, repaired, or documented through an approved partner.
```

This should not be a chaotic giveaway. It should feel like a controlled ritual.

Requirements before announcement:

- venue capacity confirmed
- RSVP flow ready
- terms written
- donation/repair/upcycle partner confirmed
- queue lead assigned
- security assigned
- content consent process ready

Kill if:

- no partner is confirmed for collected items
- the mechanism sounds like bait
- capacity cannot be controlled
- it distracts from the product

## City Clue Trail v0

Working concept:

Use permissioned partner locations to place small VORG-EAVY cards or displays.

Each clue should reveal one thing:

- object detail
- launch date
- pop-up signal
- founder table clip
- city waitlist QR

Approved location types:

- cafes
- studios
- campus-adjacent spaces
- barbershops/salons
- gyms
- creative offices
- photo studios
- local retail friends

Do not use:

- unauthorized posters
- public litter
- trespass placements
- fake "everyone has this" language
- location reveals that create a run or unsafe crowd

## Pop-Up Proof Wall

At the pop-up, build a real-time proof wall:

- `150 planned units`
- SKU/unit cards
- sold
- held
- available
- next-city signup QR

Rules:

- Sold means paid.
- Held means held.
- Available means available.
- Never blur those categories.
