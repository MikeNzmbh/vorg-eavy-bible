## Executive Decision

**Working launch window: November 5-12, 2026.**

- October is not the active plan unless approved pre-production samples, landed quotes, vendor-backed lead times, and campaign proof are already complete by the relevant purchase-order cutoff.

- The current state authorizes quotes, samples, fit work, proof content, price testing, and storefront preparation. It does not authorize bulk production.

- Initial inventory / production spend must remain below C$5,000-C$6,000 unless the founder changes the cap.
- The founder-stated product system contains five product lines: The Firm Jacket, women's low-rise denim, men's denim, scarf, and women's top / bodysuit.

- The old report's market-size evidence did not prove VORG demand.
- No vendor-backed landed COGS, MOQ, production lead time, approved pre-production sample, or reconciled size curve is currently recorded for all five product lines.

### Variant Control

The corrected plan uses approximately 22 sellable variants before the size curve is locked:

- jacket: 1 colour x 4 sizes = 4 variants
- women's denim: 1 wash x 6 sizes = 6 variants
- men's denim: 1 wash x 5 sizes = 5 variants
- long-sleeve: 1 colour x 5 sizes = 5 variants
- scarves: 2 colours x 1 size = 2 variants

Do not convert these into purchase quantities until sample measurements and price-revealed size selections exist. The unit curve should follow evidence, not even distribution.

## Price Architecture

- **Jacket:** C$249 is the working test because C$199 at C$85 landed produced only 57.3% product gross margin. If the sample cannot earn C$249, reduce landed COGS or units; do not hide the problem inside blended margin.
- **Denim:** C$128 is a working price test near full-price Canadian heritage denim. VORG must earn it through fit, wash, material, construction, model measurements, and a credible return promise.
- **Long-sleeve:** C$68 preserves strong product margin while remaining below the current Canadian SKIMS reference price. The product still needs a VORG-specific reason to exist beyond being a lower-priced lookalike.
- **Scarf:** C$35 stays below the founder's C$35.99 ceiling and reads more deliberate than a mass-retail `.99` price. It should be a pop-up entry product and cart add-on, not a paid-acquisition hero.

## Campaign Proof Gate

Before a bulk purchase order:

1. Run at least three physical-sample content tests: product-first, founder-first, and city-first.
2. Record at least 40 deduplicated, price-revealed product + size + colour selections across the drop.
3. Require at least eight qualified selections for every product entering bulk.
4. Approve at least two campaign tactics with linked evidence under the Drop OS rules.
5. Record objections: price, fit, opacity, warmth, styling, shipping, and return questions.
6. Build the unit curve from the selections and fit evidence.

The 40-selection threshold is an internal cash-risk policy, not an external fashion benchmark. Likes, unpriced polls, duplicate screenshots, and compliments do not count as purchase intent.

| Sell-through | List revenue | Economic profit after committed spend + fees | Cash after C$8,412 committed plan + fees | Cash if all C$10,000 is consumed | Unsold inventory at cost |

At 85% sell-through with 10% revenue leakage from returns, discounts, refunds, or allowances, modeled economic profit falls to about **C$1,087**. Cash after the committed plan falls to about **C$530**; if every reserve dollar is consumed, cash falls to about **-C$1,058**.

## Ecommerce Conversion System

### Discovery

- product-first campaign hooks
- founder fit and material education
- Ottawa/Gatineau connector attribution
- waitlist and SMS capture with product, size, colour, and city fields

### Product Detail Page

Every fitted product requires:

- front, back, side, movement, close-up, and outfit images
- garment measurements and model measurements
- fit notes written in plain language
- denim shown on at least two relevant bodies
- composition, construction, care, and country / supplier disclosure where applicable
- delivery estimate and return summary visible on the product page
- stock state and low-stock wording tied to real inventory only

### Cart And Checkout

- test a C$150 free-shipping threshold; denim + scarf naturally crosses it
- offer Ottawa/Gatineau local pickup
- use the scarf as a no-discount cart add-on
- support guest checkout, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay
- QA tax, shipping, discount, inventory, failed-payment, and sold-out states

### Lifecycle

- waitlist launch sequence
- browse, cart, and checkout recovery
- post-purchase fit / care message
- delivery and return status communication
- review and customer-content request after delivery
- Drop 002 / next-city capture on sold-out and post-purchase states

## Production GO Gate

Every active product must have:

- supplier and landed quote evidence
- confirmed MOQ and lead time
- approved sample and measurements
- approved pre-production sample before bulk
- size curve backed by fit evidence and price-revealed selections
- product-level margin after payment, packaging, return allowance, and shipping treatment
- compliant fibre-content and dealer-identity label plan
- customer-facing product imagery that shows the real sample, not concept art

The overall drop must also have:

- production spend inside the C$5,000-C$6,000 cap
- at least two verified campaign tactics
- tracked coverage for the 86-order 85% sell-through requirement
- storefront, policy, analytics, checkout, local pickup, and inventory-state QA
- pop-up venue, insurance, staffing, capacity, POS, and photo-consent controls

## Kill / Hold Rules

- Kill the one-block unisex jean shortcut unless independent male and female fitting proves it.
- Hold the white long-sleeve if flash opacity, recovery, and size-level demand are weak.
- Hold a second jacket colour until a later drop or a real price-revealed waitlist justifies it.
- Hold any product whose landed cost cannot support its tested retail price after variable costs.
- Hold bulk production if quotes, PP samples, campaign receipts, or the vendor-backed launch calendar are missing.
- Delay the launch rather than release goods that fail QC or misstate materials, fit, scarcity, or availability.
