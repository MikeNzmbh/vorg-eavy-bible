## Measurement Contract

### Qualified Action

Count only one of these, deduplicated by person where possible:

- consented email or SMS signup with product/size/city intent
- pop-up RSVP or fitting reservation
- PDP visit followed by size guide, shipping, returns, or add-to-cart activity
- direct product question about price, size, availability, material, delivery, or purchase
- purchase or paid reservation once checkout is legitimately live

Likes, raw views, generic compliments, and follower growth are context, not qualified actions.

### Minimum Record

Every experiment log contains:

```text
experiment ID
owner
start/end timestamp
prerequisites approved
audience/source
asset or station
impressions/reach where available
landing sessions
qualified actions by type
consented opt-ins
orders/reservations when live
cash spent
hours spent
content assets usable
complaints/incidents
decision and reason
```

### Decision Vocabulary

- `Adopt`: repeated evidence supports putting the mechanism into Drop 001.
- `Adapt`: signal exists but the execution or conditions must change.
- `Retest`: sample is too small or tracking failed; no win is claimed.
- `Reject`: the mechanism missed its threshold or violated a guardrail.

One seven-day result can advance a tactic; it cannot prove a universal law.

## X01 - Comment Becomes The Ad

Ledger link: `E01`

Suggested accountable owner: founder/campaign lead; assign by name before Day 1.

### Hypothesis

A founder video that answers one real objection with the garment, paired with a page that continues the same answer, will produce more qualified actions than aesthetic-only content.

### Prerequisites

- Credible sample or final garment in hand.
- Every product, fabric, fit, price, and timing claim approved as true.
- One tracked landing page with consent language and source links/UTMs.
- At least ten real objections from comments, DMs, fittings, or direct interviews. Anonymize them.

### Primary Decision Metric

Qualified-action rate:

```text
unique qualified actions / unique landing sessions
```

Secondary: cost per qualified action for the one promoted winner; three-second hold, completion, saves, product questions, and page depth are diagnostics.

### Seven Days

| Day | Action | Required output |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rank ten objections by frequency and purchase impact | Top five with evidence source |
| 2 | Script five 15-30 second answers; build matching page modules | Claim-approved scripts/modules |
| 3 | Shoot all five in one controlled session | Five raw answers plus detail shots |
| 4 | Publish objection 1 and 2 organically | Tagged links and baseline capture |
| 5 | Publish objection 3 and 4; reply to real questions | Comment/DM log |
| 6 | Publish objection 5; promote only the organic winner at up to C$15/day | One approved paid cell or no spend |
| 7 | Compare intent, page behaviour, and spend; decide | Adopt/adapt/retest/reject record |

### Win, Guardrail, Kill

- Win: at least two objection assets beat the current median qualified-action rate by 25% or more, at least ten qualified actions occur across the sprint, and the paid cell—if used—stays at or below the founder-approved cost per qualified opt-in.
- If no reliable baseline exists: mark the sprint `baseline established`, not `won`, and use the top two treatments in a second test.
- Guardrail: no comment identity is exposed without permission; every answer matches sample/final truth.
- Kill: stop paid delivery at C$30 total, on the first misleading claim, or when cheaper traffic produces worse product intent.

### Assets Earned

- Five objection clips.
- Five matching PDP/landing modules.
- One ranked objection bank for email, SMS, fitting, and staff scripts.

### Risk And Approval

Risk: `Green`. Founder approves claims, final edit, page, and any paid spend.

## X02 - The Firm Referral Ladder

Ledger link: `E09`

Suggested accountable owner: CRM/growth owner with founder approval; assign by name before Day 1.

### Hypothesis

Achievable non-inventory rewards and visible progress will turn existing consented supporters into qualified waitlist acquisition without devaluing the product.

### Prerequisites

- Public waitlist page with explicit email/SMS consent choices.
- Unique referral link and duplicate/fraud handling.
- Reward liability capped and deliverable.
- Rules page that states timing, eligibility, verified referral definition, reward limits, privacy, and no purchase requirement.

Proposed ladder for the test:

| Verified referrals | Reward |
| ---: | --- |
| 3 | Digital Drop 001 product dossier |
| 5 | Reserved pop-up fitting slot |
| 10 | Numbered VORG-EAVY print |
| 20 | Optional founder portrait session |

No free garment is promised in the test.

### Primary Decision Metric

Verified referred opt-ins per seeded member, with referral quality checked through product/size/city intent.

Secondary: seed-to-share rate, reward liability per qualified lead, fraud/duplicate rate, and unsubscribe/complaint rate.

### Seven Days

| Day | Action | Required output |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Approve rules, rewards, consent copy, and fraud definition | Signed test brief |
| 2 | Build and test unique links, confirmation, progress, and analytics | Five end-to-end test records |
| 3 | Seed publicly plus up to 30 existing consented subscribers | Seed log; no imported addresses |
| 4 | Show genuine aggregate progress and clarify questions | Public update with no fake social proof |
| 5 | Publish one reward demonstration and one product-proof asset | Two reusable assets |
| 6 | Remind only people who consented to receive the reminder | Suppression/consent check |
| 7 | Audit referrals, liability, lead quality, and complaints | Decision record |

### Win, Guardrail, Kill

- Win: at least 20% of seeded members share, at least nine verified referred opt-ins arrive from the 30-person seed, and referred leads equal or exceed direct leads on product/size/city intent.
- Guardrail: the referred person reaches the public page and consents directly. VORG does not upload or message a friend's address supplied by someone else.
- Kill: more than 10% suspected fraud/duplicates, any consent complaint, reward liability above C$25 for the test, or signups with materially weaker product intent.

### Assets Earned

- Referral landing/progress flow.
- Product dossier.
- Reward demonstration asset and rules template.

### Risk And Approval

Risk: `Yellow`. Founder and compliance owner approve the exact flow. CASL requires the relevant consent basis, identification, and unsubscribe; its referral exception is narrow, so this design captures consent on the landing page rather than sending unsolicited referral messages.

Reference: [CRTC CASL FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm).

## X03 - Traveling Firm Jacket

Ledger link: `E10`

Suggested accountable owner: campaign lead/sample custodian; assign by name before Day 1.

### Hypothesis

A visible seven-person custody chain around one hero sample will create stronger local product intent and more varied fit/context proof than one conventional seeding post.

### Prerequisites

- Credible, wearable jacket sample that can survive seven wears.
- Seven connectors plus two backups selected for context fit, not follower count.
- Written sample custody, cleaning/care, disclosure, content permission, transport, and return agreement.
- Unique QR/URL for each carrier.
- Founder retains the right to stop the relay after any fit, safety, or garment issue.

### Primary Decision Metric

Qualified product questions plus consented waitlist actions attributable to the seven carriers.

Secondary: completed handoffs, fit insights, usable assets, referral chain completion, sample condition, and creator hours/cost.

### Seven Days

| Day | Carrier context | Required proof |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Founder handoff and construction context | Custody record; object detail asset |
| 2 | Campus/young creative context | One honest fit note and tracked action |
| 3 | Work/founder context | One price/value objection and response |
| 4 | Cafe/studio context | Day styling and real product question |
| 5 | Movement/commute context | Comfort/movement note; no unsupported durability claim |
| 6 | Nightlife/event context | Night styling and permissioned reaction |
| 7 | Return to founder; chain recap | Sample inspection, map, evidence review |

The actual people and contexts can change. The chain remains capped at seven days.

### Win, Guardrail, Kill

- Win: at least five completed handoffs, ten qualified product questions, three consented reusable assets from different contexts, two explicit purchase-intent statements, and no material sample damage.
- Guardrail: no scripted praise. Gift/compensation is disclosed. A carrier can decline posting without penalty beyond returning the sample.
- Kill: missing custody record, unsafe transfer, garment damage affecting truth, undisclosed commercial relationship, or two consecutive carriers fail to make the product legible.

### Budget Cap

Up to C$35 for tracked local transport, cleaning, and small print/QR materials. No paid post is included.

### Assets Earned

- Seven-day chain map/recap.
- Three or more context assets.
- Fit, movement, and objection notes for product/page refinement.

### Risk And Approval

Risk: `Yellow`. Founder approves each carrier, the custody terms, usage rights, disclosure, and final recap.

## X04 - Pay With Originality

Ledger link: `E13`

Suggested accountable owner: event lead with a separate consent/content lead; assign both by name before Day 1.

### Hypothesis

A short originality action with a guaranteed, transparent benefit will qualify the right community, produce brand-native content, and convert more people to a fitting/RSVP path than a passive invitation.

### Test Mechanic

Each participant shows or performs up to 30 seconds of original work. Completion—not subjective quality—unlocks:

- a direct-flash portrait with the VORG object or set,
- a short founder styling/fit conversation, and
- a fitting-priority card or RSVP action.

There is no random draw, ranking, public humiliation, or claim that participation wins a garment.

### Prerequisites

- Permissioned, accessible room for 12-20 people.
- Posted capacity, completion rule, benefit, recording policy, privacy treatment, and no-purchase statement.
- Participant release with separate choices for attending, being recorded, and public brand use.
- Credible sample/display objects and a private alternative for participants who do not want to perform publicly.

### Primary Decision Metric

Percentage of completed participants who take the fitting, RSVP, consented opt-in, or product-page action.

Secondary: attendance, completion, content permissions, product questions, benefit cost, wait time, and participant feedback.

### Seven Days

| Day | Action | Required output |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Write the rule, benefit, consent choices, capacity, and accessibility path | One-page terms |
| 2 | Recruit 12-20 people through consented/public channels | RSVP list and source |
| 3 | Build a 60-minute run-of-show; test QR and release | Timed rehearsal |
| 4 | Shoot benefit example and product context | Two non-participant demo assets |
| 5 | Run one controlled session | Participant/action log |
| 6 | Deliver promised portraits; request separate publication approval | Delivery/permission log |
| 7 | Measure action, cost, sentiment, and product relevance | Decision record |

### Win, Guardrail, Kill

- Win: at least eight people complete, 40% or more take a qualified product/RSVP action, at least four assets receive public-use permission, and no one reports unclear rules or coercion.
- Guardrail: every completer receives the stated benefit; creative quality is not judged.
- Kill: unclear benefit, consent failure, participant discomfort, queue above 15 minutes, accessibility failure, or content overwhelms the product.

### Budget Cap

Up to C$75 for room/partner contribution, portrait delivery, and printed fitting cards. No store credit or free product is authorized in this test.

### Assets Earned

- Originality-action recap.
- Founder/participant portraits with explicit rights.
- Product-question and community-language bank.

### Risk And Approval

Risk: `Yellow`. Founder approves terms, venue, consent, participant handling, and every public asset. Legal review is required before converting this into a chance-based contest or material store-credit program.

## X05 - Pop-Up Proof Engine Rehearsal

Ledger link: `E20`

Suggested accountable owner: event lead with an independent observer; assign both by name before Day 1.

### Hypothesis

A rehearsed sequence of product proof, fit, objection resolution, checkout/opt-in, and real inventory status will outperform a beautiful but passive room—and expose operational failure before launch day.

### Test Stations

1. Arrival and consent/RSVP check.
2. Founder Table: object, construction, correction, honest tradeoff.
3. Fit Proof: size, measurement, movement, and confidence.
4. Objection Station: price, delivery, returns, difference.
5. Action Station: waitlist/SMS/RSVP or test checkout.
6. Inventory Wall: rehearsal cards clearly marked `SIMULATION - NOT SALES`.
7. Next-City Signal: one verified city signup.

Nothing from the simulated sold/held/available state may be published as customer or sell-through proof. At the live event, `sold` means paid, `held` means actively held under a stated policy, and `available` means available.

### Prerequisites

- Draft venue/floor plan or permissioned substitute room.
- Credible samples and approved claims.
- QR tracking, consent capture, size guide, inventory-card protocol, and staff script.
- Twelve invited testers representing customers and operators; they are told it is a rehearsal.
- One observer who does not run a station.

### Primary Decision Metric

Percentage of testers who reach a qualified action without staff rescue or material confusion.

Secondary: time by station, queue, fit confidence, unanswered objections, QR failures, inventory-state accuracy, accessibility, and reusable non-deceptive assets.

### Seven Days

| Day | Action | Required output |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw flow and assign one job to every station | Floor/flow draft |
| 2 | Build scripts, size tools, QR paths, and simulation cards | Station kit |
| 3 | Tabletop staff run; inject five failure cases | Failure log and fixes |
| 4 | Recruit/confirm 12 testers and consent boundaries | Attendance plan |
| 5 | Run 60-90 minute rehearsal and observe silently | Time/action/error log |
| 6 | Fix the two highest-friction stations | Revised kit/flow |
| 7 | Run a six-person verification pass or walkthrough | Launch decision record |

Failure cases to inject: wrong size request, return question, QR failure, held-versus-sold dispute, and checkout/internet failure.

### Win, Guardrail, Kill

- Win: at least 75% reach a qualified action, median station queue stays under five minutes, every inventory state is correct, at least 80% can explain the product/fit/action afterward, and all five injected failures have a documented response.
- Guardrail: rehearsal status is explicit; simulated demand is never used publicly.
- Kill: inventory-state error, unsafe flow, inaccessible critical station, failed consent capture, no offline checkout contingency, or product questions staff cannot answer truthfully.

### Budget Cap

Up to C$50 for printed station tools, QR/inventory cards, tape/wayfinding, and tester hospitality. Venue spend is not authorized by this card.

### Assets Earned

- Verified station kit and staff scripts.
- Objection/failure log.
- Consent and inventory-state protocol.
- Safe shot list for the live event.

### Risk And Approval

Risk: `Yellow`. Founder approves room, participants, scripts, consent, simulated-state labelling, and any asset published from the rehearsal.

## Launch Gate

The edge portfolio can enter the live Drop 001 campaign only when:

- at least one product-proof creative mechanism repeats a qualified-action win,
- the waitlist flow records valid consent and source,
- sample fit and claims are defensible,
- every public scarcity/inventory state can be reconciled,
- each physical tactic has venue, safety, accessibility, and consent controls,
- costs remain inside the founder-approved cap, and
- a tactic has an owner, rollback, and kill authority.

Do not delay fundamental sample, vendor, unit-economics, storefront, or fulfilment work to manufacture spectacle.
