# Launch

## Launch Format

Open direct-to-consumer online drop plus one controlled day-to-night pop-up.

The online launch should not be password-gated. Scarcity should come from controlled units, waitlist/SMS preheat, release timing, local seeding, pop-up RSVP control, and sold-out archive.

## Drop 001 Target

- Working launch window: November 5-12, 2026, conditional on vendor-backed production, demand-proof, inbound, and QC gates
- Active product set: The Firm Jacket, women's low-rise denim jean, men's denim jean, scarf, women's top / bodysuit
- Initial inventory / production spend ceiling: C$5,000-C$6,000 max
- Planned units: TBD after vendor quotes
- Target revenue: TBD after prices and units are rebuilt
- First-drop goal: sell-through plus reusable content and waitlist growth
- Growth-system target: 85% sell-through inside 30 days; 45% growth floor / 50% target on eligible scale steps; approximately 40-day cadence to an August 2027 C$100k net-sales month. This does not pre-approve production above the C$5,000-C$6,000 first-buy ceiling.

## Tone

Luxury restraint breaking into social pressure.

Daytime should feel sparse, controlled, and premium. Night should feel alive, direct-flash, local, and access-driven.
