# VORG Omnichannel Demand System — U.S.-First, Instagram Included

Checked: 2026-08-22
Status: operating architecture and order-coverage goals; not historical traffic, a CAC benchmark or spend authorization.
Market: U.S.-first; Brooklyn operating lead hypothesis; Chicago, Los Angeles and Atlanta co-finalists until first-party evidence separates them. Canada follows Ontario → Quebec → Vancouver → Halifax.

## Decision supported

How VORG grows enough qualified demand to support 85% sell-through in 30 days, then compounds eligible releases 45%-50% every 40 days without becoming dependent on TikTok alone.

## Portfolio principle

VORG needs five connected jobs, not a list of apps:

1. **Make desire:** Instagram, TikTok, creators, founder media and editorial imagery.
2. **Capture intent:** Google, Pinterest, TikTok Search, Shop search, product/size waitlists.
3. **Close trust:** Shopify PDP, fit proof, shipping/duties truth, checkout and reviews.
4. **Create repeat:** email, SMS, post-purchase, referral, community and seasonal rhythm.
5. **Produce real-world proof:** controlled pop-up, stylist pulls, campus/cultural connectors, PR and customer content.

One piece of content may travel across several surfaces, but each acquired order is counted once. Assists are kept in a separate table.

## Instagram system

Instagram becomes a first-class lane, not a TikTok repost folder.

### Reels — discovery

- Run Trial Reels where available to test new creative with non-followers first.
- Score on qualified profile/PDP visits, shares, saves, DMs and downstream orders—not reach alone.
- Create four recurring franchises: `The Piece`, `The Decision`, `The City`, `The Original`.
- One clean product-proof Reel, one founder/story Reel and one native social Reel per weekly creative batch.
- Promote only assets that preserve on-site intent and retained contribution.

### Carousels — consideration and saves

- Slide 1: a decisive look or tension.
- Slides 2–4: fit, material, movement and styling.
- Slide 5: founder/product reasoning.
- Slides 6–8: detail, size/price/shipping truth and one next action.
- Measure saves, shares, product taps, time-to-PDP and assisted conversion.

### Stories — launch control room

- Product/size poll → waitlist capture → proof progress → countdown → stock-by-size → customer receipts.
- Use questions and DMs as structured research; tag product, market, size, price reaction and objection.
- Never manufacture countdown scarcity or hide delivery/returns truth.

### DMs and creator collaboration

- DMs are service and research, not a spam channel.
- Creator collaboration posts remain governed by contracts, material-connection disclosure and usage rights.
- Reuse rights, paid amplification period, territory and music clearance must be explicit before boosting.

## TikTok system

- Organic is the rapid-format lab: hook → proof → close.
- Search is a separate intent lane using customer language, product attributes, styling questions and problem queries.
- Shop/LIVE is a marketplace decision, not a default. Build a TikTok-specific catalog and price only after fees, return handling, contribution, inventory reservation and brand-control gates clear.
- Creator marketplace tools may support discovery, but creator selection uses audience/fit, comments, delivery reliability and customer quality—not follower count.
- Commercial music and branded-content disclosures receive a pre-publish check.

## YouTube founder system

YouTube makes the founder and garment logic durable.

Recurring programs:

- `Building The Firm` — the real weekly operating story.
- `Why This Piece Exists` — price, fabric, construction and silhouette.
- `The Sample Table` — what passed, what failed and what changed.
- `City Proof` — Brooklyn-led community, styling and permitted pop-up evidence.
- `The Drop Debrief` — receipts, customer feedback and next decisions.

Long-form video is cut into Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikToks, Pinterest clips, PDP modules, email sections and founder stills. YouTube's native title/thumbnail test is used where eligible; a no-winner result is accepted.

## Creator / UGC portfolio

Use a portfolio, not random gifting:

- 40% product/fit communicators;
- 25% culture/city connectors;
- 20% visual world builders;
- 10% non-fashion adjacent creators (music, design, photography, food/nightlife where credible);
- 5% experimental voices.

Every seeded or paid relationship records: why selected, audience/market hypothesis, compensation/gift, disclosure instruction, content deliverables, deadline, usage/whitelisting rights, music, unique link/code, units sent, content returned, orders after returns, customer quality and kill/renew decision.

No scripted praise, fake UGC, undisclosed gift or follower-based revenue forecast.

## Google, Pinterest and Shop: the quiet compounding layer

### Google

- Shopify → Merchant Center feed.
- Product/variant schema, price, availability, size/gender/product type, shipping, returns and images reconciled.
- Free listings before paid Shopping.
- Search Console and Merchant Center query/product diagnostics logged weekly.
- Paid non-brand Search/Shopping begins only after feed approval, PDP truth and contribution ceiling.

### Pinteres

- Catalog plus boards built around real visual/customer language, not generic mood-board spam.
- Clean vertical product, styling, detail, founder process and seasonal-search assets.
- Evergreen pins point to the exact PDP or editorial landing, with tag/API health checked.

### Shop app / conversational product discovery

- Accurate titles, descriptions, images, price, availability and shipping market.
- Shoppable posts and post-purchase follow-up where eligible.
- Do not create a separate brand truth; Shop inherits Shopify's canonical catalog.

## Email, SMS and waitlists

Build compounding flows before constant newsletters:

1. welcome / brand-world orientation;
2. product and size-interest capture;
3. sample/proof progress;
4. launch sequence;
5. browse/cart recovery with frequency limits;
6. order/fit/care education;
7. review and customer-content request;
8. restock/next-season interest;
9. win-back based on product ownership and cohort.

Email and SMS need separate, provable consent. Canada-facing commercial messages must follow CASL consent, identity and unsubscribe requirements; U.S. SMS requires vendor/legal review for TCPA and state obligations. A referral form captures the referred person's own consent instead of automatically messaging uploaded contacts.

## Ecommerce close layer

Every acquisition promise must continue on the landing/PDP:

- one decisive product promise;
- clean front/back/detail/movement imagery;
- fit model measurements and garment measurements;
- size selection help and later fit/return learning;
- material, care and construction truth;
- price, USD/CAD market, duties/taxes and delivery truth;
- returns and exchange terms before checkout;
- relevant complete-the-look modules;
- fast mobile performance;
- Shop Pay/accelerated checkout where appropriate;
- GA4/Shopify purchase/refund/variant instrumentation.

## Paid growth gate

Paid starts as distribution of proven creative, not a separate identity.

1. Creative clears a qualified-intent threshold organically or in a tiny controlled test.
2. PDP/mobile/checkout and tracking are verified.
3. Maximum CAC is derived from retained contribution, not an industry average.
4. Founder approves platform, market, total cap, daily cap, creative, landing and kill rule.
5. Marginal CAC and contribution are read by spend tier; blended ROAS cannot hide deterioration.
6. Scale in evidence-sized waves; no unattended spend agent.

## 85% sell-through scoreboard

- Day 3: product truth and distribution operating; no broken catalog/checkout.
- Day 7: velocity by SKU/size and channel; intervene on creative or fit questions, not price panic.
- Day 14: target ≥50% trajectory; replenish option can be prepared, not committed.
- Day 21: target ≥70% trajectory; resolve dead size/return warning.
- Day 30: target ≥85% settled; positive retained contribution; next 45%-50% step may become eligible.
