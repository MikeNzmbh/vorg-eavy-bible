# Product

## Drop 001 SKU Architecture

1. The Firm Jacket
2. Structured Rib Top / Bodysuit
3. Signature Cap

## Product Logic

- The jacket establishes hierarchy and visual authority.
- The top creates a lower-friction entry point.
- The cap creates accessible proof of belonging.

## Hero Product

The Firm Jacket should be a structured cropped or waist-length uniform jacket with a strong collar, controlled branding, and a premium shell fabric path.

Avoid broad true-leather runs until demand is proven.
