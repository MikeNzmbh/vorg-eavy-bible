# Barrel Jean Supplier Brief

Checked: 2026-06-24

## Decision Supported

Request comparable factory quotes and a first fit prototype for an original VORG-EAVY unisex barrel jean, using Zara relaxed barrel jeans as the current silhouette direction and Uniqlo E479000 only as secondary public measurement/reference context.

## Current Truth

- Style code: `VE-BJ-001`.
- Working name: Barrel Jean Concept.
- Truth level: `concept/specification draft` for RFQ and proto only.
- Base proto size: 28.
- Intended market: Canada, beginning with Ottawa/Gatineau.
- Preferred first color: Void Black / washed black.
- Preferred fabric direction: rigid or low-stretch cotton denim, 11-13 oz after wash. Cotton/lyocell remains an option if the supplier can prove handfeel, recovery, shrinkage, and cost.
- Updated fit direction: loose hip and thigh, curved barrel leg, knee pleat/dart shaping, and relaxed hem opening. Avoid tight ankle taper or jogger effect.
- This is not approved for bulk production.
- This concept is outside the active three-SKU Drop 001 architecture unless separately approved.

## Supplier Message

Hello,

We are developing an original unisex barrel-leg jean for our Canadian micro-label, VORG-EAVY. Please review the attached Excel tech pack and quote the style as an RFQ/prototype project.

Please reply in the `Supplier RFQ` sheet and confirm:

1. Whether you are a direct factory or trading company, and your factory location.
2. MOQ by style, color, and size.
3. EXW, FOB, and DDP-to-Ottawa pricing at 50, 100, 150, and 300 units.
4. Fabric options closest to non-stretch 79% cotton / 21% lyocell denim at 11-12 oz after wash, including swatch cost and mill data.
5. Sample fee and lead time for fabric swatches, one size-28 fit proto, a size set, and a pre-production sample.
6. Bulk lead time, wash-plant location, testing capability, and quality-control process.
7. Whether you can return the measured sample spec and digital pattern after the first proto.

The Zara/Uniqlo references in the workbook are reference evidence only. Do not reproduce Zara or Uniqlo labels, branding, artwork, proprietary patterns, or distinctive trim details. Develop an original pattern to the VORG-EAVY target spec and flag any pattern changes you recommend before cutting.

Please do not begin bulk production. We will approve fabric, wash, fit, graded measurements, trims, labels, testing, and a pre-production sample in writing first.

Thank you.
