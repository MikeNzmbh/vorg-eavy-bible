Please send 3-5 original or client-authorized images showing front, back, inside construction, leather-collar attachment, quilting intersections and sleeve-end finish. You may remove client logos and must not share anything restricted by a client NDA.
>
> 2. Can your pattern and sewing team execute the straight continuous no-cuff sleeve exactly as described? Please explain the proposed construction, how you will maintain the 3 cm turnback and 16 cm half opening without wrist narrowing, and the two most likely first-sample failure points plus your prevention plan.
