## Fit intent and negative ease

The garment must look deliberately small when laid flat and expand comfortably on the body. Do not substitute a standard fitted T-shirt block. Negative ease is part of the design.

`Negative ease % = (finished garment circumference − target body circumference) / target body circumference × 100`

### Base-size S working fit targets

| Zone | Target body circumference | Finished garment circumference | Negative ease | Working range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bust | 86.0 cm | 70.0 cm | −18.6% | −15% to −20% |
| Waist | 70.0 cm | 58.0 cm | −17.1% | −15% to −20% |
| High hip / hem | 90.0 cm | 72.0 cm | −20.0% | −15% to −20% |

These are prototype starting targets, not vendor-validated production specifications. The supplier must reconcile them with actual fabric stretch, recovery, modulus, and opacity. Return the measured sample specification and proposed grade nest before bulk approval.

### Fit acceptance gates

- Smooth second-skin fit with no unintended loose body volume.
- Hem finishes near high hip; approximately 3–4 cm longer than the earlier concept crop.
- Full torso coverage in a neutral standing position; controlled exposure may occur only through styling with low-rise bottoms.
- No neck gaping, shoulder drag, sleeve twisting, armhole cutting, excessive compression, or hem roll.
- Slight controlled translucency is acceptable only after on-body approval; uncontrolled sheerness is not acceptable.
- Fit must be assessed after wash and recovery conditioning, not only immediately after dressing.

## Prototype, testing, and approval gates

1. Supplier returns completed RFQ, actual fabric specification, swatches, stretch/recovery method, and proposed measured specification.
2. VORG-EAVY approves fabric hand feel, black colour, controlled translucency, logo strike-off, and construction sample.
3. Supplier makes one size-S prototype and returns a full measured-spec report.
4. Fit review checks negative ease, length, comfort, neckline, sleeve mobility, opacity, seam appearance, and post-wear recovery.
5. Wash test checks dimensional stability, spirality, colourfastness, pilling, and seam durability.
6. Revised sample is required if any critical gate fails.
7. Bulk cutting remains blocked until a signed physical sample, final graded specification, BOM, label proof, price, production timeline, and QC plan are approved.

### Required tests / declarations

- Fibre composition and fabric weight.
- Stretch, growth, and recovery in both directions, with method disclosed.
- Dimensional stability and spirality after the proposed care cycle.
- Colourfastness to wash, dry/wet crocking, and perspiration.
- Pilling/abrasion performance appropriate to a close-fitting top.
- Seam strength, seam extension, and popped-stitch trial.
- On-body opacity/translucency under daylight and direct flash.
- Restricted-substances compliance for intended Canadian sale.
- Needle/metal control and final-inspection procedure.
