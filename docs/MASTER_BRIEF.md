# VORG-EAVY Master Brief

Last updated: 2026-06-25

## Executive Summary

VORG-EAVY should be built first as a direct-to-consumer fashion micro-label. The broad marketplace idea should stay parked until there is product-market proof.

The highest-probability move is to narrow the system:

- one city
- one brand site
- one drop architecture
- one pop-up format
- one repeatable reinvestment loop

The first wedge is Ottawa/Gatineau, with University of Ottawa and bilingual social spillover as the first dense audience.

## Operating Model

VORG-EAVY should sell a world before it sells a catalog.

The public promise can remain:

`A home for Originals.`

The internal operating idea is:

`The Firm.`

The Firm means access, polish, ambition, discipline, and belonging.

## Reference Stack

The strategic reference stack is:

- Luxury restraint: lasting objects, materials, quiet spaces, craft.
- Fashion plus lifestyle: lookbook, sound, store, cafe, editorial, community.
- Youth energy: members, creative platform, emotionally expressive product culture.
- Scarcity mechanics: controlled units, late specificity, local proof, physical event pressure, and sold-out archive.

Do not copy any one brand. VORG-EAVY should synthesize the principles.

## Financial Logic

Pricing should carry:

- landed COGS
- variable ops
- fixed launch cost allocation
- founder salary load
- profit cushion

Current working assumptions:

- Planned units: 150
- Founder salary load: C$3,000
- Salary load per unit: C$20
- Fixed non-inventory launch cost per unit: about C$21.33
- Variable ops per unit: about C$11.15

Each unit needs to recover about C$52.48 above landed COGS before true profit begins.

## January 2027 Gate

Do not treat self-employment as realistic unless all four conditions are true:

| Gate | Minimum threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Sell-through quality | 2 launches at 85%+ sell-through within 21 days |
| Revenue quality | trailing 90-day average revenue of C$15k+/month |
| Margin quality | trailing 90-day net after founder salary load of C$4k+/month |
| Cash quality | next PO funded plus 3 months personal runway |

If these are not true, the correct move is to keep the job and continue compounding.

## Launch Playbook

The launch should feel like luxury restraint breaking into social pressure.

Assets:

- hero trailer
- teaser
- product cutdowns
- seeding stills
- pop-up recap
- sold-out board

Launch mechanics:

- open DTC storefront at launch
- pre-launch waitlist/SMS capture
- seeded local connectors
- controlled day-to-night pop-up
- strong recap content
- immediate Drop 002 waitlist capture

## Pop-Up Blueprint

The first pop-up should use two modes in one room:

- daytime: hospitality-led, sparse, premium
- nighttime: access-led, social, content-heavy

Version one should avoid:

- alcohol
- haze
- open flames
- uncontrolled crowd stunts
- vague permit assumptions

Use an indoor venue already approved for retail or assembly use.

## Website Direction

Shopify remains the recommended starting platform unless a specific blocker appears.

Required states:

- pre-launch waitlist/SMS capture
- public launch
- sold-out archive/waitlist

Core content:

- full-bleed hero media
- Drop 001 module
- lookbook strip
- The Firm waitlist module
- journal/archive module
- shipping, returns, sizing, policies

## Product System

The hero product is The Firm Jacket:

- structured cropped or waist-length jacket
- strong collar
- coated cotton twill, waxed canvas, or premium synthetic leather first
- true leather saved for later pre-order or micro-capsule
- minimal external branding
- landed COGS goal around C$85-C$110

If bodysuit fit risk remains high, switch the second SKU to a structured rib tee or long-sleeve.

## Risk Posture

The first cycle should preserve cash, learn quickly, and avoid legal/operational mess.

Main risks:

- sample delays
- QC issues
- low waitlist
- venue or permit confusion
- crowd management
- high returns
- payment/checkout issues
- HST threshold surprise
- music licensing gaps

Each risk should have a concrete trigger and contingency.
